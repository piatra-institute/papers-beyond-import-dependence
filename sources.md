## Considered and dropped

- **The Chinese journal article behind the seed's "100,000 components, 5,000 suppliers"**: the supplier count is verified at first hand from the manufacturer's annual report, so the primary record is cited instead; the component count is unverified and dropped.
- **The seed's EUV prototype timeline (2028 target, 2030 likely)**: unnamed sources; the attributable March and July 2026 reports carry the status instead.
- **ASML's 208 metrology and inspection systems**: unconfirmed, dropped.
- **Hirschman (1958), linkages**: the linkage idea is carried by Lall and by Bell and Pavitt.
- **Bloom and Van Reenen, management practices**: adjacent, not load-bearing.
- **Ministry of Industry and Information Technology statements**: the Five-Year Plan recommendations carry the policy point with a firmer record.
- **The seed's regional score table and 0-to-4 codebook**: priors and instruments rather than findings; the paper states the coding requirements in prose and asserts no regional scores.
- **The seed's panel specification against eight historical shocks**: the empirical programme the framework implies, described but not claimed as executed.
